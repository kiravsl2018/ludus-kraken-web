Tattoo artists in Estepona

One shared standard

Nanopusher and Cordero Tattoo make up the Ludus Kraken team. Both tattoo every style we do in the studio: realism, portraits, dotwork, fine line, colour, lettering, cover-ups and touch-ups. That is why you do not need to choose an artist: we book you in according to the studio’s schedule and your piece gets the same care either way.

We work with original designs, single-use equipment and one client per room. Before the session we go through the drawing with you and explain how long it will take and what it will cost. If you are coming from Marbella, San Pedro, Manilva or Casares, you will find us in the centre of Estepona, on Calle Miguel de María Luque.
