Appointments in Estepona

How to book your appointment

To book at Ludus Kraken, our tattoo studio in Estepona, tell us your idea in the form: placement, approximate size and whether you have reference photos. When you send it, WhatsApp opens with the message ready, you attach your photos in that same chat and we reply with a fixed quote and the available dates.

We are open Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 14:00 and 17:00 to 20:30, always by appointment. We do realism, fine line, colour, lettering, cover-ups and touch-ups, and the session of around four hours costs €280 until the end of the year. You can also call us on 663 78 24 77. We welcome clients from Estepona, Marbella, San Pedro, Manilva and Casares.
