Aftercare

Your tattoo, day by day

Full healing takes about four weeks. We give you this guide in writing at the end of the session.

First few hours

Protection

  • Keep the film or dressing on for as long as we tell you.
  • Wash with lukewarm water and mild soap, without rubbing.
Days 1 to 3

Moisturising

  • Pat dry with clean kitchen paper.
  • A thin layer of healing cream, two or three times a day.
Weeks 1 and 2

Peeling

  • It may itch and peel: do not scratch.
  • No sun, pool, sea or sauna.
Month 1

Healed

  • The skin settles and the colour sets.
  • Use high-factor sunscreen so it lasts.
Frequently asked questions

Before your appointment

If your question is not here, call or message us on 663 78 24 77 and we will get back to you.

How much does a tattoo cost?

It depends on the size, placement and detail. We give you a fixed quote based on your idea. For large pieces we currently offer a session of around four hours for €280.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. We work by appointment so we can give your piece the time it needs. Book on WhatsApp or with the form on our booking page.

Can I bring my own design?

Of course. We go through it with you and adapt it to the placement so it ages well on the skin.

Do you do cover-ups and touch-ups?

Yes. Send us a photo of the tattoo you want to cover or refresh and we will tell you the options and how many sessions it needs.

Does it hurt a lot?

It depends on the placement and on each person. Ribs, feet and the inner arm tend to be more uncomfortable. We take breaks whenever you need them.

How should I prepare for the session?

Sleep well, eat before you come and avoid alcohol the day before. Wear comfortable clothes that leave the area free.

What if I am under 18?

You must have signed consent from a parent or legal guardian. Please contact us before booking.
