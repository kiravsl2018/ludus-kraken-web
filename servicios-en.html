How we work

From idea to skin

Four steps, no rush. You will always know what we are going to do, how long it takes and what it costs.

  1. i.

    Consultation

    Message us on WhatsApp with your idea, the placement and the size. If you have references, send them over.

  2. ii.

    Custom design

    We draw the piece for you and fit it to your body. You get a quote and a date before we start.

  3. iii.

    Session

    Come rested, fed and hydrated. Large pieces are done in sessions of around four hours.

  4. iv.

    Aftercare

    • Wash with mild soap and lukewarm water.
    • Apply a thin layer of healing cream.
    • No sun, pool or sea while it heals.
    • Do not pick the scabs.
